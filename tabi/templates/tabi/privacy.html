Privacy & ethics

Tabi is designed for meaningful connection around interests, not popularity. Feed ranking does not use follower or like counts.

Anonymous posting hides your identity from other members. Authorized administrators can trace account links when reviewing safety reports. Do not include identifying details in anonymous content.

Profile images accept JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 5 MB. Messages are available to conversation participants and site administrators with database access.

Tabi is a university project. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information.
